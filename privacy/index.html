Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

BoxBuddy ("we") helps you track packages. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@boxbuddy.net.

What we collect

DataWhyWhen
Account idTo keep your packages and settings under your accountAlways (guest accounts get one too)
Email address and nameTo identify your accountOnly if you sign in with Apple, Google or an email code (Apple may give us a private relay address)
Packages: tracking numbers, carrier, names and destinations you enterTo track them and show their statusWhen you add a package
Shipping emails from a connected Gmail accountTo find tracking numbers automaticallyOnly if you connect Gmail; see Gmail below
Subscription statusTo unlock BoxBuddy PlusIf you subscribe
Push notification tokensTo send delivery alerts and Live Activity updatesIf you allow notifications
Usage events, crash reports and diagnostic logsTo understand which features are used and fix bugsWhile you use the app

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Usage events are tied to your account id only. They never include your email address, name, tracking numbers, package names, addresses or email contents.

Gmail

If you connect Gmail, BoxBuddy reads messages from shipping senders to find tracking numbers and the sender's name (for example "Amazon"). We store the tracking number, carrier and sender name, not the email itself. You can disconnect Gmail in Settings at any time, which revokes our access with Google.

BoxBuddy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by people except where you ask us to for support, where required for security, or where required by law.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that run BoxBuddy for us:

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • EasyPost and carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL): receive tracking numbers to return their status.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store payments.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions (account id and purchase history).
  • PostHog: product analytics, crash reports and diagnostic logs (account id and usage events).
  • Google: only if you sign in with Google or connect Gmail. Signing in with Google does not give us access to your mailbox.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete account (Settings > Delete account) erases your packages, settings, connected mailboxes and sign-in account right away. Analytics records tied to your account id are deleted on request (see below).
  • Guest accounts that haven't been used for 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • Turning off delivery alerts or signing out removes this device's notification token.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or object to analytics, by emailing support@boxbuddy.net. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

BoxBuddy is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
