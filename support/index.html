Frequently asked questions

Which carriers does BoxBuddy support?

USPS, UPS, FedEx and DHL.

Do I need an account?

No. BoxBuddy starts you with a guest account. Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code from Settings to keep your packages if you change phones. Guest accounts that go unused for 90 days are deleted.

My package status isn't updating.

Carriers sometimes take a day to show a new tracking number. Check that the number and carrier are correct, then pull down on the package list to refresh. If it's still stuck, email us the carrier name (not the tracking number) and we'll look into it.

How does Gmail scanning work?

Email scanning is part of BoxBuddy Plus. If you connect Gmail, BoxBuddy reads emails from shipping senders to find tracking numbers. We keep only the tracking number, carrier and sender name, not the email. You can disconnect Gmail in Settings at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I get with BoxBuddy Plus?

The free plan tracks up to 3 active packages. Plus gives you unlimited packages, live updates, earlier delivery notifications and automatic email scanning. It's billed through your Apple ID as a monthly or yearly subscription.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or see Apple's guide. To restore Plus on a new device, sign in to the same BoxBuddy account and tap Restore purchases on the Plans screen. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I'm not getting delivery notifications.

Make sure notifications for BoxBuddy are on in iPhone Settings and that delivery alerts are on in BoxBuddy's Settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account. This erases your packages, settings, connected mailboxes and sign-in account right away. If you can't access the app, email support@boxbuddy.net from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings.
